# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all participants in the DDF community to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be patient and understanding
- Focus on constructive criticism
- Respect differing opinions and experiences
- Give credit where it is due

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insults
- Unwelcome sexual attention or comments
- Hate speech or slurs
- Publishing others' private information
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at open@arkstride.com.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Violations of this code of conduct may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the community.
