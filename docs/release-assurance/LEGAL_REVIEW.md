# Legal Review Gate

Required review should address, as applicable:

- open-source license and third-party notices
- trademark/branding
- privacy/data handling representations
- security and production-readiness claims
- cryptographic/evidence claims
- non-repudiation wording
- customer contract language
- warranty/disclaimer language
- export/sanctions considerations
- subprocessors and managed-service terms if applicable

The approval must identify the reviewer, date, reviewed commit/release, and any
conditions or prohibited claims.

DDF automation cannot substitute for qualified legal review.
